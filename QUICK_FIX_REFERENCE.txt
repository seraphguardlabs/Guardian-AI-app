## ❌ PROBLEM #1: Risk Alert Section Not Visible

### **Where Was It?**

- Supposed to show real alerts but wasn't visible

### **What Was Added?**
- ✅ New full-width "Risk Alerts" section
- ✅ Shows top 5 most recent alerts
- ✅ Displays:
  - **🕐 Time** when alert was detected (e.g., "5m ago")
  - **📊 Risk Score** (0-100 scale)
  - **🎯 Severity** (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW with colors)
  - **📝 Summary** (what was detected)
- ✅ Clickable → tap to see full alert details
- ✅ Real-time updates as new alerts arrive

### **How to See It?**

## ❌ PROBLEM #2: AI Service Status Not Visible

### **Where Was It?**
- Child Screen
- No way to know if AI models were loading/running

### **What Was Added?**
- ✅ New "AI Service Status" card at TOP of child screen
- ✅ Shows **real-time model loading progress**:
  - 🟡 Initializing
  - 🟡 Loading BERT Model
  - 🟡 Loading LSTM Model
  - 🟡 Loading Vision Model
  - 🟢 Running (all models ready)
- ✅ Displays:
  - **✅/❌ Models status** (checkmarks when loaded)
  - **🔄 Background monitoring** (Active/Inactive)
  - **📊 Models running** (0-3/3)
  - **⏱️ Last analysis time** (when models last ran)
- ✅ **Color coded**:
  - 🟢 Green = All running
  - 🟠 Orange = Loading
  - 🔴 Red = Error
- ✅ Test button to manually verify

### **How to See It?**
1. Open app as Child (on another device)
2. **First card at the top** = AI Service Status
3. Watch real-time updates as models load
4. See green checkmarks ✅ appear one by one
5. Tap "Test" button to manually test AI services
